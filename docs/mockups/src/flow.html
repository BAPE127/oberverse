HOW TROOPS AND BATTLES WORK — the whole loop in six steps (mock-up)

Everything below reads and writes the same saved military record. A soldier, a tank or a missile exists once, in one place, in one state.
1

People are hired and trained, squads are made, and kit comes from the right place. Nothing is spawned for you.
