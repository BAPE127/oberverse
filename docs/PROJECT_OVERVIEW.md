# Project Overview

## What the Oberverse is

A browser-based, persistent, character-driven sandbox RPG. The player lives in an original semi-modern seaside borough, Lanternside, among residents who have schedules, personalities, moods, memories, relationships and limited knowledge. Problems have several solutions that come from shared systems (keys, ownership, witnesses, relationships, reputation, items, time), and the world keeps going when the player is elsewhere.

Inspirations are high-level only (systemic freedom and emergent situations in the spirit of immersive-sim sandboxes such as *Streets of Rogue 2*). No content, assets or distinctive mechanics are copied.

## Design priorities (in order)

1. A stable, playable foundation.
2. Consistent, meaningful systemic interactions.
3. Believable NPC behaviour and character-specific mechanics.
4. A world that supports exploration and emergent stories.
5. Persistent progression and consequences.
6. Strong visual and audio identity.
7. Expandable architecture and maintainable documentation.
8. More content and complexity.

## Pillars

- **Characters first.** Personality fields must change behaviour or dialogue, or they don't belong in the data.
- **Outcomes, not scripts.** Objectives describe what must become true; the systems supply the ways.
- **Plausible knowledge.** Nobody knows something without a channel (saw it, heard it, was told, read it, it's their job).
- **Recoverable mistakes.** Failure changes the world but rarely closes it off (confessing, apologising, next day's job).
- **Combat is optional.** Planned as one system among many; never the only way through ordinary content.
- **Offline and private.** No server, paid API or AI service is needed to play.

## Scope of 0.1.0

A compact but dense area (one outdoor map, six interiors), three original residents, three quests/jobs, five emergent event templates, original audio, durable saves, tests and documentation. See `CHANGELOG.md` and `docs/KNOWN_ISSUES.md` for exact coverage.
