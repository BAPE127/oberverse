# Prompt 20 — Extend the corporate empire

Use when working on the companies, groups, managers, markets, central bank, bonds, loans or geopolitics (the staged programme in `docs/CORPORATE_AUDIT.md`), or when any business needs to move money.

Read first: `docs/CORPORATE.md`, `docs/CORPORATE_AUDIT.md`, `src/systems/corporate.js`, `src/corp/*.js`, `src/content/corporate.js`, and the business you touch (`docs/ARMS.md`, `LOGISTICS.md`, `AEROSPACE.md`, `FARMING.md`).

Rules:
- **All company money goes through `game.corp`** (`pay`, `receive`, `canAfford`, `cashOf`, `found`). Never write `player.money` for a business again; share trades, dividends and other personal money stay on the wallet.
- A budget, a valuation, a ranking or a consolidated figure is **never cash**. Funding is an explicit, logged act; a funded company that cannot pay refuses (or owes, for a bill that must be met) — nothing is received for nothing.
- Generated entities (companies, later candidates and managers) are made **once** from a saved seed, stored in full, and never regenerated; randomness is a hash of the seed and the id, never the shared RNG.
- Figures come from the books each business keeps (`metricsOf`); a figure not kept is `null` and shown as "—". Never fabricate to fill a table.
- Migrations grant nothing: no money, shares, loans, businesses, employees; nothing is auto-consolidated. Test with `tests/fixtures/save-v0.59.0-four-businesses.json` (a real pre-v0.60 save).
- Keep each stage independently testable, commit each part green, and gate payment-layer changes on the existing suite passing **unedited**.
- Managers (stage 7) act only through validated services within budgets and permissions; the owner chose that they may also build or buy from **pre-approved plans** (each asset type needs an explicit placement rule).
- The group: money to a subsidiary only through the parent; transfers are never trading income and must keep the total cash unchanged; whatever is internal to the group is eliminated in consolidation (and a charge a buyer has capitalised stays deferred until it is used); monthly jobs are keyed by month and must run once under save/load.
- People (v0.62): a person is one job at one employer, paid from that employer's own account once a day, and does nothing while unpaid; an effect is a bounded change to money actually paid or output actually made, applied at the point of payment or production, and zero without the thing it acts on; nobody hired must change nothing (prove it by running the previous release beside the new one); skills grow from results only; authority is data until a stage enforces it.
- Managers (v0.63): a decision is quoted (no side effects), judged by the one pure function, then executed through the business's own service, queued or refused with a reason; no budget means no spending; only companies with their own account; approving judges again; an owner's own spending never counts against a manager's budget; with no budget set the game must behave exactly as the previous release (prove it by running both).
- Bids and expansion (v0.64): a bid is an owner-set margin over the quoted cost (never below cost, below the margin waits for the owner); an expansion is a plan with limits and a fixed pure placement rule, bought through the business's own service from its own account; new permissions default to false; nothing happens without a margin or a plan.
- Treasury (v0.65): cash moves only along the group's own transfers (never sales or costs, total cash unchanged), by a policy the owner set; the source account is checked with the reserve left; approval re-judges and moves what is needed now.
- Exchange (v0.66): a share's price is a pure function of the company's own books and a hash mood — never of the player's trades and never the game RNG; fees, caps and dividends are the same for every listing; a failed company's shares are worthless and a takeover pays out once; the older shares' behaviour changes only on purpose, with a test saying so.
- When a screen states how an older system behaves, read that system's code first: v0.66 said Chuckie's Cheese had no fee or cap and it always had both.
- Central bank (v0.68): the policy rate is a pure function of the world's own indicators, decided once a month, written down, never set by the player; every effect is exactly identity at the neutral rate, so the game before the first meeting is unchanged.
- Bonds (v0.69): a bond is a list of dated cash flows priced at the current yield (par at issue, falling when rates rise); each coupon is paid exactly once whatever is saved or slept through; nothing is created before a purchase.
- A new interior for an existing building must be the building's grown footprint (make it after `growStreets`, like `phonographLobby`); a larger one moves the town and every hand-placed coordinate in the content.
- Corporate bonds (v0.71): default is the world's own closing of a company, never a dice roll; the rating is a pure function of the books; the bond market must not change a company's records.
- A new building or home must not move a street: make a hall or room the exact footprint (and door) of what it replaces (`hallSize`, `firstBankInterior`, `lariatBankInterior`) and compare every building's position before and after. A moved street breaks saves.
- A consequence of default is whatever the owner chose, no more: do not add a clever extra (a cash sweep, a seizure) because it feels natural. If a design needs one, ask.
- A bond issuer's trouble comes from a number that moves every day for a reason a player can read (`causes()`); never from a die. Anything that can default is driven there by a counter of days, not a roll.
- A fact about the world that a stored record captured when it was made (a tender's embargo flag, a price quoted yesterday) must be re-read when it is acted on, or it goes stale: ask the live source (`A.embargoed(customer)`), not the copy.
- An event's economic effect is a pure function of its saved record and the day (`src/corp/geo.js`): never apply a shock once, in a tick, or from a handler — that is how it repeats on a reload.
- A decision that spends money on growth (a hire, a bid, an expansion) passes `frozen` to `judge` when the company is in arrears to the bank; a new kind of manager decision decides whether it is "non-essential" in `src/corp/management.js`.
- Anything that sells a holding the player may have pledged (shares, bonds) asks `game.corp.lending.checkRelease({ kind, value, rating? })` first and returns its refusal unchanged; the borrowing record in `world.corp.lending` is its own thing and must never be read from, or written by, anything about crime, reputation or valuations.
- A new tablet app goes in a group in `src/ui/tabletGroups.js` and keeps a unique name; `openPanel('tablet', { tab })` is how every other part of the game opens it.
- Deliver: tests (rules, persistence, old-save load), docs, a browser check of every screen touched, and an honest report of what is and is not done.
