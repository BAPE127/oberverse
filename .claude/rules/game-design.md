# Game design rules

The vision is in `docs/GAME_DESIGN.md`; the setting in `docs/WORLD_BIBLE.md`; the plan in `docs/ROADMAP.md`. The owner decides direction. Do not change an established mechanic, character, place or figure the owner set without asking.

- Open world, no chapters, no forced path. Politics, war and business are opt-in.
- Outcomes, not scripts: objectives say what must become true; shared systems supply the ways. Combat is never the only way through ordinary content.
- NPCs live in the world: a home, a job, a schedule, relationships, limited knowledge gained through a channel.
- Businesses are connected: supply, production, transport, sales, accounts, the exchange. No screen that the wider economy cannot feel.
- Losses in war are permanent. Failure changes the world but rarely closes it.
- Imported characters follow the owner's interview (`docs/CHARACTER_IMPORT.md`) and are protected (the Wrath).
- Original content only: inspired by systemic sandboxes such as Streets of Rogue 2 at a high level, never copying content, assets or distinctive mechanics.
- Offline and private: no server, paid API or AI service in gameplay.
